How It Works

Up and running in less than 2 minutes.

1

Create Application

Navigate to the Discord Developer Portal, create a "New Application", name it whatever you want Discord to say you are playing, and copy the Application ID.

Developer Portal
2

Add Images

Under the Rich Presence tab, upload your Art Assets and name them simply (e.g., "logo"). Or skip this step and just use direct URLs inside Nebula!

3

Launch Status

Open Nebula Presence, paste your ID, fill out the text boxes, and hit "Start Presence". Your new custom profile is now live for everyone to see.
